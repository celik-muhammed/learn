Incident Management & On-Call
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

* `Google SRE Workbook: Incident Response
  <https://sre.google/workbook/incident-response/>`_
  (covers structured incident response, roles, mitigation-first workflows, and postmortems)
  by Google SRE

* `PagerDuty Ops Guides: Incident Response Guide
  <https://docs.pagerduty.com/ops-guides/incident-response-guide>`_
  (covers on-call expectations, severity levels, incident roles, crisis response, and postmortems)
  by PagerDuty

* `What is a Runbook? (Runbook Design & Automation)
  <https://www.pagerduty.com/resources/automation/learn/what-is-a-runbook/>`_
  (covers runbook structure, operational checklists, and automated response workflows)
  by PagerDuty
