-----------------------------------
Progression Overview
-----------------------------------

This directory organizes learning and inquiry tracks from foundational concepts
through specialized engineering, graduate and doctoral scholarship, frontier
research, and open scientific or possibility spaces:

.. list-table:: Curriculum Tiers (101–999)
   :widths: 12 28 60
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Tier
     - Track
     - Focus & Scope
   * - **101**
     - :doc:`101-foundational/index`
     - Core introductions, environment setup, and entry-level AI/ML frameworks.
   * - **201**
     - :doc:`201-intermediate/index`
     - Agent orchestration, stateful workflows, structured outputs, and post-training.
   * - **301**
     - :doc:`301-advanced/index`
     - Distributed scaling (FSDP), PEFT/alignment, production agents, and AI security.
   * - **401**
     - :doc:`401-specialized/index`
     - High-throughput inference serving, compiler/kernel tuning, and risk governance.
   * - **501**
     - :doc:`501-graduate/index`
     - University lecture series, distributed systems foundations, and graduate texts.
   * - **601**
     - :doc:`601-research/index`
     - Preprint archives, peer-reviewed proceedings, and research lab publications.
   * - **701**
     - :doc:`701-doctoral/index`
     - Archival journals (JMLR, PMLR), representation learning venues, and interpretability.
   * - **801**
     - :doc:`801-postdoctoral/index`
     - Open peer-review forums, statistical ML archives, and longitudinal ecosystem reports.
   * - **901**
     - :doc:`901-frontier/index`
     - Frontier model safety, compute/scaling trajectories, and generalization benchmarks.
   * - **999**
     - :doc:`999-scientific/index`
     - Open mathematical, computational, physical, biological, astro, and possibility spaces.
